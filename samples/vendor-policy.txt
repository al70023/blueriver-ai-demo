==============================================================================
1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
==============================================================================

This policy defines the requirements that must be satisfied before any
external vendor is authorized to perform work on behalf of BlueRiver Field
Services or before any vendor invoice is approved for payment. It applies
to all vendors providing equipment, materials, subcontracted labor, or
professional services to BlueRiver, regardless of contract size.

==============================================================================
2. VENDOR APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS
==============================================================================

A vendor must satisfy all of the following requirements before being
classified as Approved Status in the vendor management system:

2.1 W-9 ON FILE
    A current IRS Form W-9 must be on file. The W-9 must match the legal
    business name and tax identification number used on the vendor's
    invoices.

2.2 INSURANCE DOCUMENTATION
    The vendor must provide a current Certificate of Insurance (COI) with
    the following minimum coverage levels:

      - General Liability:        $1,000,000 per occurrence
      - Aggregate Limit:          $2,000,000
      - Workers Compensation:     statutory limits per state of operation
      - Auto Liability:           $1,000,000 combined single limit (vendors
                                  operating vehicles on BlueRiver sites)

    BlueRiver Field Services must be named as an Additional Insured on the
    General Liability policy. The COI must list a current effective date
    and an expiration date no less than thirty (30) days in the future at
    the time of invoice approval.

2.3 SIGNED MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
    A countersigned Master Services Agreement (MSA) must be on file.
    Purchase Orders or Statements of Work referencing the MSA may then
    govern individual engagements.

2.4 BANKING INFORMATION VERIFICATION
    For ACH payments, banking information must be verified through a
    callback to a known phone number on the vendor's W-9 or MSA. Email
    confirmation alone is not acceptable due to documented vendor email
    compromise risk in the industry.

==============================================================================
3. INVOICE APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS
==============================================================================

An invoice may be approved for payment only when ALL of the following
conditions are met:

3.1 Vendor must be in Approved Status (Section 2).
3.2 Vendor's COI must be current and not expired.
3.3 Invoice must reference a valid Purchase Order or Service Agreement.
3.4 Work or materials referenced in the invoice must be confirmed received
    by the assigned BlueRiver project owner.
3.5 Invoice line items must match the Purchase Order within an acceptable
    variance (5% on labor, 0% on materials unless documented in writing).

If any of conditions 3.1 through 3.5 are not met, the invoice must be
placed in HOLD status pending resolution. An invoice in HOLD status must
not be entered into QuickBooks until the missing condition is satisfied.

==============================================================================
4. APPROVAL TIERS
==============================================================================

Invoices are approved according to the following tiers:

  Tier 1 - up to $2,500       Operations Manager
  Tier 2 - $2,501 - $10,000   Operations Manager + Department Head
  Tier 3 - $10,001 - $50,000  Department Head + CFO
  Tier 4 - over $50,000       CFO + CEO

==============================================================================
5. EXCEPTIONS
==============================================================================

Exceptions to any provision of this policy require written approval from
the CFO. Exception approvals must be documented in the vendor management
system with a reason code and an expiration date.

Emergency vendor engagements (defined as work required to prevent imminent
property damage, safety hazard, or material business disruption) may
proceed under a temporary approval with a 14-day window to bring the
vendor to full Approved Status. Any invoice resulting from emergency
engagement must still satisfy Section 3 requirements before payment.

==============================================================================
6. REVIEW AND AUDIT
==============================================================================

The Operations Manager reviews vendor approval status quarterly. Any
vendor whose insurance documentation expires within the next 30 days is
flagged for COI renewal outreach. Vendors that fail to provide updated
documentation are moved to HOLD status until documentation is received.

The full vendor master is audited annually. Vendors with no activity in
the prior 24 months are archived.
